title: 📢 Call for Submissions - FaSE4Games’26 @ FSE 2026 (Montreal, Canada)
---

We are excited to announce the 3rd ACM International Workshop on Foundations of Applied Software Engineering for Games (FaSE4Games’26) and invite contributions from the community!

FaSE4Games brings together researchers, practitioners, and educators working on the challenges of software engineering for entertainment games, serious games, and gamified applications. The workshop offers an open and collaborative environment to share new ideas and discuss emerging topics in game development.

🔍 We welcome:
• Long papers (8 pages, incl. references)
• Extended abstracts (5 pages, incl. references)

All submissions will be reviewed by three reviewers. Accepted contributions will be presented at the workshop.

📅 Important Dates
• Submission deadline: February 12, 2025
• Notification: March 19, 2025
• Camera-ready: April 2, 2025

🌐 More information: http://fase4games.quest

Whether you submit a paper or simply join us for the event, FaSE4Games offers a welcoming space to learn, share, and connect with the community driving software engineering for games.

See you in Montreal! 🇨🇦
