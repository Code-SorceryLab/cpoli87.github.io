From 5 to 19 August 2026 I am visiting the [Department of Computer Science (DCC)](https://dcc.ufmg.br/) at the Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG), in Belo Horizonte, Brazil.

The visit is hosted by Prof. [João Eduardo Montandon](https://dcc.ufmg.br/professor/joao-eduardo-montandon-de-araujo-filho/), coordinator of the project _Improving Software Development Productivity with Large Language Models_ (CNPq nº 444507/2024-8), and has three goals: strengthen the research collaboration between our groups, deliver a seminar for graduate students and faculty, and hold focused meetings with faculty members to advance the project's research lines.

## The seminar

I gave a talk titled **Games, Software Engineering & AI — What we build, break and measure in our lab**, covering the work we do at Code & Sorcery: how we build games, how we break them, and what we measure along the way.
