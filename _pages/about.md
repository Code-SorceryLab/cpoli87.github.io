Hi, my name is Cristiano Politowski.

I am an Assistant Professor at [**Ontario Tech University**](https://ontariotechu.ca/), within the Computer Science group in the Faculty of Science.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral researcher with Professor [**Houari Sahraoui**](https://www.iro.umontreal.ca/~sahraouh/) and Professor [**Michalis Famelis**](https://michalis.famelis.info/) at the [Software Engineering Research Group (GEODES)](https://geodes.iro.umontreal.ca/), part of the Department of Computer Science and Operations Research (DIRO), at [**Université de Montréal**](https://www.umontreal.ca/), Montréal, Québec, Canada.

Additionally, I worked as a Postdoctoral researcher with Professor [**Ghizlane El-Boussaidi**](https://www.etsmtl.ca/programmes-et-formations/corps-enseignant/gelboussaidi) at the Département de génie logiciel et des TI at [**École de Technologie Supérieure (ÉTS)**](https://www.etsmtl.ca/), Montréal, Québec, Canada.

I completed my Ph.D. under the supervision of Professor [**Yann-Gaël Guéhéneuc**](https://explore.concordia.ca/yann-gael-gueheneuc) at [Ptidej](https://www.ptidej.net/) and co-supervision of Professor [**Fabio Petrillo**](https://fabiopetrillo.com/), at the Department of Computer Science and Software Engineering at [**Concordia University**](https://www.concordia.ca/), Montréal, Québec, Canada.

My research interests include _Video Game Development_, _Software Testing_, _Artificial Intelligence for Software Engineering (AI4SE)_, _Deep Reinforcement Learning_, and _Empirical Software Engineering_.
