Generative NPCs can contradict themselves, reveal knowledge the character should not have, absorb unwanted bias, or simply stop being the person they were written as. We are developing a structured way to probe those failures deliberately rather than waiting for players to find them, and to score a character's responses along several dimensions of consistency.
