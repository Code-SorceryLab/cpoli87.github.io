As a conversation grows, a model's attention drifts from the persona it was given toward the history it has accumulated, and the character slides. Prompting harder is not a durable fix. We are looking at ways to hold authored traits steady from within the model, with an emphasis on the smaller architectures that are realistic to actually ship inside a game.
